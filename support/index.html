Support

Help with Dewpoint

Support covers installing the theme, its settings and sections, the data model, bugs and update questions for stores that use Dewpoint. Replies usually arrive within one working day.

Read the docs first

Most questions are answered in the documentation, including the troubleshooting list and the notes on what each section needs before it renders.

Report a bug

Include your store address, the page, the browser and a screenshot. Bugs are fixed in theme updates at no charge.

Customization

Anything that can be done with a setting, a section or a metafield is covered. Custom code is quoted separately.

Contact

Use the form below, or email mohsinshah53@gmail.com. If the question needs a look inside your theme editor, send a collaborator request for the store; a password is never asked for.

Dewpoint is an original theme and is not affiliated with Shopify. For billing, orders, payments or your Shopify account, contact Shopify Support.

Updates

Theme Store purchases receive updates through Shopify's Themes → Update flow. Release notes are in the changelog. Updating keeps your settings and section content; it does not change your metafields or metaobjects.
